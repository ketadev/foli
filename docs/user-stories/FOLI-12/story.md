# FOLI-12 · Recover a lost streak — MVP expansion

**As a** reader, **I want to** have a gentle chance to recover my streak, **so that** I can return to reading after a break.

**Acceptance criteria**

- If a streak ends, a recovery challenge appears with its goal and progress clearly shown.
- Completing the goal restores the previous streak and continues it; the same day is never counted twice.
- If I do not complete the challenge, I can keep reading and build a new streak without a block or a punishing message.
- The challenge's duration and deadline are defined and tested before this story is implemented.

**Depends on:** FOLI-11. **PRD:** §12; the PRD gives three consecutive days as an example but does not define every rule.
