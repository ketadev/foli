# FOLI-06 · Choose a page goal — Core

**As a** reader, **I want to** choose how many pages to read in this session, **so that** the goal fits my time and energy.

**Acceptance criteria**

- I can choose 5, 10, or 15 pages, or enter a custom positive number.
- The app does not automatically choose the amount for me; I see my chosen goal before starting.
- The goal cannot exceed the pages remaining in the book; the limit is explained.
- The session goal stays attached to the session even if I read more or fewer pages.

**Depends on:** FOLI-03. **PRD:** §§7, 9.
