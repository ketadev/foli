# FOLI-03 · Manage books to read and books in progress — Core

**As a** reader, **I want to** distinguish the books I am reading from those I plan to read, **so that** I know what to continue next.

**Acceptance criteria**

- I can see books in the Reading and To Read states and move a book from To Read to Reading.
- If two books are already in Reading, I cannot activate a third; the app explains why and keeps the library consistent.
- I can move a book from Reading to To Read without losing its current page or sessions.
- The state remains the same after closing and reopening the app.

**Depends on:** FOLI-01. **PRD:** §8.1.
