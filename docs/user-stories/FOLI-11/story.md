# FOLI-11 · View the reading streak — MVP expansion

**As a** reader, **I want to** see how many consecutive days I have read, **so that** I can stay consistent without feeling pressured.

**Acceptance criteria**

- A day with at least one valid session counts once, even if I read multiple books or complete multiple sessions.
- The streak is calculated from the sessions' local reading dates and persists after restarting the app.
- The streak appears in the session result and on the home screen when applicable.
- Rules for time zone changes and dates around midnight are defined and tested before this story is implemented.

**Depends on:** FOLI-08. **PRD:** §12.
