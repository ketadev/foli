# FOLI-13 · Earn small achievements — MVP expansion

**As a** reader, **I want to** recognize specific reading milestones, **so that** I can feel progress without competing with anyone.

**Acceptance criteria**

- Achievements for the first session, first week of reading, seven consecutive days, reaching 25% and 50% of a book, and the first finished book are granted only once.
- When ideas and actions exist, the first saved idea and first applied idea are also recognized.
- I can view each achievement later; its presentation is brief and does not block the reading flow.
- There are no leaderboards, comparisons, or automatic public posts.

**Depends on:** FOLI-08, FOLI-10, FOLI-11; the last two achievement types also depend on FOLI-15 and FOLI-18. **PRD:** §§13, 22.
