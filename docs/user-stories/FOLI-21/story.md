# FOLI-21 · Keep the experience private and calm — Cross-cutting

**As a** reader, **I want to** use Foli without an account or public exposure, with a calm interface, **so that** I can focus on the reading habit.

**Acceptance criteria**

- I can use the library, session, progress, ideas, and actions flows without registering.
- Reading data is stored locally; the only optional transmission of a note is the one in FOLI-20 after I request a suggestion.
- There are no communities, public profiles, followers, leaderboards, or automatically shared activity.
- Screens prioritize the book and the next action, use the project's design tokens, and keep celebrations brief.

**Depends on:** a cross-cutting requirement for every delivery wave. **PRD:** §§4, 21, 22.
