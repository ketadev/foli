# FOLI-04 · Change a book's reading intention — MVP expansion

**As a** reader, **I want to** change a book's reading intention, **so that** the experience fits what I want from that book.

**Acceptance criteria**

- I can switch among the three intentions after adding the book.
- The change affects the options shown in future sessions without deleting saved ideas or actions.
- I can always read and save progress, regardless of the intention.

**Depends on:** FOLI-01. **PRD:** §6.
