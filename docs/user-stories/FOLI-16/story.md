# FOLI-16 · View a book's ideas — MVP expansion

**As a** reader, **I want to** revisit the ideas I saved for a book, **so that** I can recall what mattered to me.

**Acceptance criteria**

- I can open the ideas for a book I am reading or have finished and read their text.
- Ideas persist after restarting the app and remain available offline.
- Global search and classification by idea type are not required.

**Depends on:** FOLI-15. **PRD:** §§14, 23, 25.
