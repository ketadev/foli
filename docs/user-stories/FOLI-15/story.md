# FOLI-15 · Save an optional idea — MVP expansion

**As a** reader, **I want to** save an idea after reading when something interests me, **so that** I can remember it later.

**Acceptance criteria**

- After completing a session, I can choose “Save an idea” or “Not now”; either choice leaves the session complete.
- I can write an idea linked to the book and, when applicable, to the session.
- The MVP uses a single “Idea” type and does not require me to classify it as a quote, note, or reflection.
- I can close or leave the form without losing my reading session.

**Depends on:** FOLI-08. **PRD:** §§4, 14.
