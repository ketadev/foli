# FOLI-14 · Set a considerate reminder — MVP expansion

**As a** reader, **I want to** choose a reminder time and be able to turn it off, **so that** I receive help returning to reading without too many notifications.

**Acceptance criteria**

- I can turn a local reminder on or off and choose its time.
- Notification permission is requested when I enable the reminder; if I deny it, reading still works.
- Changing the time or turning the reminder off updates or cancels the scheduled notification without duplicates.
- At most one habit reminder is scheduled per day, with brief text and without sharing reading data with third parties.

**Depends on:** FOLI-05. **PRD:** §20. **Architecture:** native Android/iOS adapters.
