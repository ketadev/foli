# FOLI-10 · Finish a book and view reading history — Core

**As a** reader, **I want to** have the app recognize when I finish a book, **so that** I can celebrate and keep it in my reading history.

**Acceptance criteria**

- When a valid session reaches the final page, the book is marked Finished and a brief celebration appears.
- The book no longer occupies one of the two active slots and remains available in the finished books history with its full progress.
- If a final reflection is offered, I can skip it without affecting session completion or the book's state.
- The book and its sessions and ideas are preserved when it moves to history.

**Depends on:** FOLI-08, FOLI-03. **PRD:** §19.
